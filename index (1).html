About me

I'm a Quality Improvement Specialist at UT Health Science Center in San Antonio, where I analyze reports, keep data accurate across patient systems, and flag process issues so teams can fix them.

My path started in biology (B.S., UTSA) and moved into computer science (B.S., Oregon State), and I'm now deepening the math behind it with a master's in Applied and Computational Mathematics at Johns Hopkins.
